My research deals with the mathematical construction and study of 2D conformal field theories, with a focus on Liouville and Toda CFTs. This involves techniques from probability theory (Gaussian fields, Gaussian multiplicative chaos, random geometry, diffusions...), vertex operator algebra (Virasoro/W-algebras and their representations), differential and complex geometry (Riemann surfaces, uniformization problems, holomorphic vector bundles...), as well as strong inputs from theoretical physics (conformal quantum field theory, statistical physics).
